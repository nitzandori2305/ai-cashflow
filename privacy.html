Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This policy covers the "AI Cashflow" publishing application (the "App"), an internal tool operated by the owner of the AI Cashflow YouTube channel. The App is not offered to the public and is used only with the channel owner's own Google account.

What the App accesses

  • Through the YouTube Data API: uploading videos, setting thumbnails and editing video metadata on the owner's own channel.
  • Through the YouTube Analytics API: read-only statistics of the owner's own channel.

The App does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or viewer.

How data is stored and protected

The OAuth tokens that authorize the App are stored on a private server, readable only by the service account that runs the App, and are never shared with third parties. Channel statistics are used only to plan the channel's own content.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
